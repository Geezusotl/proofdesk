# Onboarding

## The first day

New employees receive their laptop and account details on the first day. The IT desk can be reached at it@brindlelabs.example. Each new hire is assigned a buddy, a colleague outside their team who answers questions for the first three months.

## Choosing a laptop

New hires choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a ThinkPad X1. Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if they fail. The choice is made before the start date so the machine is ready.

## The first week

Security training must be finished within the first 5 days. In the same week the manager shares a 30-60-90 plan that describes goals for the first three months. Every new hire also receives an otter plush toy as a welcome gift.

## Probation

The first 90 days of employment are a probationary period. A review takes place at the end of the first month and again at the end of the third. Either side can end the contract with one week's notice during probation.

## Payday

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. The first payment is pro-rated to the days worked.
