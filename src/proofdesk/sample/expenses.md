# Expenses

## Submitting an expense

Expenses are submitted through Ledgerly within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the purchase are not reimbursed. A receipt is required for any single item over $25.

## Approvals

A manager can approve expenses up to $500. Anything above $500 and up to $5,000 needs a director, and anything above $5,000 needs a vice president. Approvers cannot approve their own expenses.

## When you are paid back

After approval, reimbursements are paid within 10 business days, in the same currency as the receipt where possible.

## Meals and entertainment

Client dinners are reimbursed up to $120 per person. Meals for team events are reimbursed up to $35 per person. Alcohol is reimbursed only at client dinners and never counts toward the team event limit. Meals while travelling follow the per-diem rule in the travel policy instead.

## Software and equipment

Software must be bought through the IT desk and never on a personal card, so that licences stay under company control. Equipment over $1,000 needs a director's approval before purchase.

## Mileage

Business trips in a personal car in the United States are reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and the regular workplace is not reimbursed.
