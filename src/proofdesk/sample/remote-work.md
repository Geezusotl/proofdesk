# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Office days

Employees who live near an office work in a hybrid pattern. The core in-office days are Tuesday and Thursday, and the rest of the week can be worked from home. Wednesdays are meeting-free for everyone, in the office or not.

## Fully remote work

Working fully remotely is allowed with the approval of a director. Fully remote employees are expected to visit an office, or join the team gathering, at least twice a year. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time, and everyone should be reachable during them.

## Home office setup

New remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair or monitor. The stipend can be used once, and receipts are submitted through Ledgerly as with any expense. Brindle Labs also reimburses internet costs up to $40 per month.

## Co-working spaces

Remote employees who live more than 50 kilometres from an office may claim up to $150 per month for a co-working space. The space needs to be approved in advance by the manager.

## Working from another country

Employees can work from another country for up to 20 days in a calendar year. This needs the manager's approval and a sign-off from the security team, because some countries restrict the kind of customer data that can be handled there. Longer stays create tax and legal issues and must be arranged with the people team months ahead.
