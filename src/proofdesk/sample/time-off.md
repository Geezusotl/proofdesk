# Time Off and Leave

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year. Days accrue monthly, at roughly two days a month, so someone who joins in July has about half of the annual amount available during their first year. Part-time employees receive a share in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Carrying days over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire without payment. Days beyond the limit of 5 are lost on December 31.

## Requesting time off

Requests are made in the Calendula tool. Time off of five working days or more should be requested at least two weeks ahead. Managers answer requests within three business days, and a request that gets no answer in that time is treated as approved.

## Sick leave

Each employee has 10 paid sick days a year, separate from paid time off. A doctor's note is only needed after more than 3 consecutive sick days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Company holidays

Brindle Labs observes 11 company holidays a year, including Otter Day on the first Friday of June and the week between Christmas and New Year's Day. The full list is published each January in Calendula. Employees working in an office in another country follow the local public holidays in addition to Otter Day.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver of a new child receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave can be taken at any point within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption, and it may be split into two blocks.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave after the death of an immediate family member and 2 days for an extended family member.
