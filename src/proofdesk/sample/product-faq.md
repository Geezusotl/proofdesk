# Product FAQ

## Brindle Notes plans

Brindle Notes has three plans. The free plan allows up to 50 notes. The Pro plan costs $8 per month and removes the limit. The Team plan costs $12 per user per month and adds shared notebooks and an admin console.

## Brindle Sync storage

Brindle Sync includes 2 GB of storage for free. The paid plan costs $4 per month and provides 100 GB. Files in a deleted folder are kept for 30 days before they are removed permanently.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 30 days of the first payment for any paid plan. Annual plans are refunded in full during that window and in proportion afterwards only if the service was unavailable.

## Exporting your data

Notes can be exported at any time in Markdown or JSON format from the account settings. Exports include attachments, and large accounts are sent by email as a download link.

## Platforms

Both products run on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android and in the web browser. The Linux client is in beta and does not yet support shared notebooks.

## Support

Support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC. Pro customers receive a reply within 24 hours and Team customers within 4 hours. The service targets 99.9% uptime each month.
