# Benefits

## Health insurance

Brindle Labs pays 100% of the health insurance premium for the employee and 70% for dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment and includes dental and vision.

## Wellness stipend

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of $50 per month for gym memberships, therapy apps or similar. It is paid through payroll and is separate from the home office stipend.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per year for courses, books and conference tickets. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference tickets come out of the learning budget, while the travel to the conference follows the travel policy.

## Retirement

In the United States the company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4% of salary. Employees in Portugal and Singapore are enrolled in the local pension schemes with equivalent contributions.

## Stock options

Stock options vest over 4 years with a one-year cliff. Nothing vests before the first anniversary, and after that the options vest monthly.

## Referral bonus

An employee who refers a candidate that is hired receives a referral bonus of $2,000, paid after the new hire completes their first 90 days.
