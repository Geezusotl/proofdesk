# Travel Policy

## Booking

All travel is booked through the Wayfare portal at least 14 days before departure. Trips booked later need a manager's note explaining why. Wayfare shows the policy limits next to each option, so staying within them is the default.

## Flights

Economy class is used for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class needs approval from a vice president and is only granted in exceptional cases. Unused tickets are credited to the traveller, and the credit expires after 12 months.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $220 in the tier-one cities of New York, London, San Francisco and Singapore, and $160 everywhere else. Hotels outside Wayfare are not reimbursed unless a conference organiser requires a specific one.

## Meals on the road

Travelling employees receive a per-diem of $60 per day for meals on domestic trips and $75 per day on international trips. Receipts are not needed for the per-diem. Client dinners are handled separately under the expenses policy.

## Rental cars and rides

Rental cars are limited to the compact class unless more than three people travel together. Taxis and ride-share are allowed for getting to and from airports and between meetings.

## Insurance and documents

Travel insurance is added automatically to every Wayfare booking at no cost to the traveller. For international trips the passport must be valid for at least 6 months after the return date. Lost luggage must be reported to the travel desk within 24 hours.
