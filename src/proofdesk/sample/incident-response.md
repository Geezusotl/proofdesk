# Incident Response

## Severity levels

A SEV1 incident is a full outage or any loss of customer data, and the on-call engineer must respond within 15 minutes. A SEV2 incident is a major feature being down for many customers, with a response within 30 minutes. A SEV3 incident is a minor problem and is handled by the end of the next business day.

## During a SEV1

The first responder names an incident commander, who runs the call and makes the decisions. All discussion happens in the #incident-live channel. The status page is updated every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved, even when the update only says that work continues.

## On-call rotation

On-call duty rotates weekly, and the handover happens on Mondays at 10:00 Lisbon time. Engineers receive an on-call stipend of $150 for each week of the rotation. An engineer who is paged outside working hours can start the next working day two hours later.

## After the incident

A written postmortem is due within 5 business days of every SEV1 and SEV2 incident. Postmortems are blameless: they describe what happened and what to change, not who caused it. Action items from a postmortem are tracked until they are closed.

## Data breaches

If personal data may have been exposed, the privacy officer must be told immediately. Affected customers and the regulator are notified within 72 hours of the company becoming aware of the breach.
