# About Brindle Labs

Brindle Labs makes two products, Brindle Notes and Brindle Sync, for people who want their work to follow them across devices. The company was founded in 2016 in Lisbon by Marisol Quade and Teodor Ilves. Quade serves as chief executive officer and Ilves as chief technology officer.

## Where we work

About 140 people work at Brindle Labs. The headquarters is in Lisbon, with two other offices in Denver and Singapore. Roughly a third of the staff work fully remotely from other cities.

## Our mascot

The company mascot is a purple otter named Brindle. The otter appears on the welcome gift every new hire receives, and it is the only character allowed on official slides. Designers who want to draw a new pose for it must ask the brand team first.

## Otter Day

Every year on the first Friday of June the whole company stops work for Otter Day, a day of talks, games and a shared lunch. Otter Day is a paid company holiday, and attendance in person is optional for remote staff.

## Values

Four values guide decisions at Brindle Labs: be kind, be curious, ship small, and write it down. When two values pull in different directions, the team is expected to say so openly and choose the one that protects customers.
